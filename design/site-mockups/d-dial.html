Overlay minimap for Escape from Tarkov

The whole squad, on one dial.

A round minimap that sits on top of the game. Your position and heading come from your own screenshots, your squad's from theirs, joined by a six-character room code.

Specifications

What TarTrak 0.14.5 does, in numbers and plain words.

Position source
The screenshot filename: X, Y, Z and rotation
Map detection
The game's text log, with a manual picker as fallback
Overlay
Circle with compass bezel, or box. Size, bezel and rim buttons adjustable
Rotation
North-up by default, heading-up as a toggle
Heading line
100 m, range marks at 50 / 100 / 150 m
Squad
Up to 12 players per room, joined by a 6-character code
Relay
Keeps nothing, logs nothing. You can run your own
Old markers
Full for 30 s, settle at 35% after 5 min. Never hidden
Raid timer
Read from the game's own raid clock, PMC and Scav
Map data
tarkov.dev, PvP or PvE
Accounts, telemetry
None, none

Controls

Five keys. Every one rebindable.

F5

Overlay modeSwitch between the full window and the overlay.

F6

OpacityChange how see-through the overlay is.

F7

HideHide the window until you press it again.

ALT+ drag

MoveDrag the overlay where you want it.

L ALT+PRTSC

Mark hereDrop a private marker where you stand.
