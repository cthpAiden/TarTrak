TarTrak reads this filename. Then it puts you and your squad on the map.

Every time you press PrtSc in raid, Tarkov names the screenshot after where you stand and which way you face. TarTrak turns that into a live squad map and overlay. Free and open source.

01 / 04 · POSITION

Three of those numbers are where you stand.

X and Z place you on the map in metres. Y is your height, which is how TarTrak knows you went up to the second floor.

02 / 04 · HEADING

Four more say which way you face.

The camera's rotation is stored as four numbers. TarTrak turns them into a compass heading, here 048°.

03 / 04 · MAP

Together, that's a dot and a line.

Your marker and heading line land on the map, with range marks 50, 100 and 150 m ahead. The next screenshot moves them.

04 / 04 · SQUAD

Share six characters. Their dots join yours.

One player creates a room, the rest type the code. Positions pass through a small relay that keeps nothing and logs nothing.
